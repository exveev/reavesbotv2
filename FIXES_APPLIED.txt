Fixes applied for false "username taken" results and 401 errors:

1. server/index.js no longer treats 401/403 responses as username taken.
2. /api/check now forwards authentication failures using HTTP 401/403.
3. The SSE snipe route emits auth_error and stops instead of repeatedly saying taken.
4. The captured real-native-request-token is sent unchanged instead of rewriting its prefix.
5. Axios calculates Content-Length automatically.
6. client/src/App.js displays authentication/server errors separately from availability.
7. Expired saved credentials are removed from localStorage and the reconnect modal opens.
8. Corrupt localStorage credentials are handled safely.

After deploying this version, disconnect/reconnect once with a fresh captured request.
